Sillwalker 0.1.12 is out! A new Hexing elite that can curse your hero, two new pets (Mantis and Octopus), a new quest to grow your pet, a Sky Forge rare event and a Travel Day blessing. Here is what's new since 0.1.11:

  • New elite, the Hexing: its hits can curse the hero, who then takes 15% more damage for 3 seconds (purity shortens the curse).
  • New pet, the Mantis: it raises your attack speed (up to 5% more at level 10).
  • New rare event, “Sky Forge”: for 30 seconds, every item found arrives already forged (+2).
  • New pet, the Octopus: your hits sometimes strike twice (up to a 5% chance at level 10).
  • New quest type: have your pet gain experience (a bonus egg one time in four).
  • New daily blessing, “Travel Day”: your pets’ expeditions take half as long that day.
  • New achievement: “Hex Breaker (25 hexing)”, for hunters of the new elite.

Your save carries over: just install the new version over the old one.

Thanks for playing!
