Sillwalker 0.1.5 is out! Here is what's new since 0.1.4:

  • An original soundtrack: four tracks composed for the game, one for every 50 zones ("The Sill", "Starpaths", "The Lieutenants", "The Starbreaker"), built around one theme, from warm lofi to a galactic finale. A bubble names each new track; in Settings > Sound, ◀ ▶ lets you listen to them and 📌 keeps your favourite.
  • Separate volumes: an "Effects" slider joins "Music" and "Ambience".
  • Experience you can see: a gauge under your level on the strip (it flashes gold when you level up), and in the mini mode's hover bubble.
  • Chests you open: claiming a quest chest (click, "Claim all" or the R key) opens it before your eyes; Enter equips the item if it's stronger, otherwise the window closes by itself; and it plays its little fanfare.
  • Poupouce: a white cat with black spots, extremely rare (one monster in 1,000), drops 100 times a monster's gold and a legendary item, with its own achievement, "Poupouce?!", and its own daily blessing, Poupouce Day.
  • The Colossal: an 11th kind of elite, as big as a boss: 120% more health, 40% slower blows, and an achievement for 25 wins.
  • Pet Day: a 17th daily blessing doubles the active pet's bonus.
  • A roomier Adventure tab: equipment now comes before the bag, the bag is wider (16 slots per row on large screens) and an item's details fit without scrolling; the Log moves to Statistics > Log (J key), and Settings sits left of "Mini mode".
  • Clearer: the shop says "Reach zone 40 (record 32)" (the zone counts, not the level), Settings joins the buttons on the right, the selected item gets a light frame, damage numbers no longer cover the top of the strip, and quest rewards (chest, egg, shards, elixirs) explain themselves on hover.
  • Lighter: the music is no longer synthesized live (about one point of CPU less), and the track is freed from memory when the strip stays hidden.
  • A new game no longer opens the "What's new" window over the intro; clicking the very edge of a button now counts.

Your save carries over: just install the new version over the old one.

Thanks for playing!

  • 거대한: 11번째 정예 종류, 보스만큼 큼: 체력 120% 증가, 공격 40% 느림, 25마리 처치 시 업적.
  • 펫의 날: 17번째 오늘의 축복, 활성 펫 보너스가 두 배.
  • 더 여유로운 모험 탭: 장비가 가방 앞으로, 가방은 더 넓게(큰 화면에서 한 줄에 16칸), 아이템 상세는 스크롤 없이 보입니다. 기록은 통계 > 기록으로(J 키), 설정은 "미니 모드" 왼쪽으로.
  • 더 명확하게: 상점에 "구역 40 도달 (기록 32)"이 표시되고, "설정"은 오른쪽 버튼 옆으로, 선택한 아이템은 밝은 테두리, 피해 숫자가 띠 위쪽을 가리지 않으며, 퀘스트 보상은 마우스를 올리면 설명이 나옵니다.
  • 더 가볍게: 음악을 실시간으로 합성하지 않고, 띠가 숨겨져 있는 동안에는 곡을 메모리에서 해제합니다.
