Sillwalker 0.1.9 is out! A new daily blessing for ascensions, a twelfth kind of elite that hits harder as it weakens, two new pets, a new kind of quest and a clearer mini mode. Here is what's new since 0.1.8:

  • New daily blessing, “Star Day”: +50% star shards on ascension; that day, a ✨ follows the gain shown in the Ascension tab.
  • New kind of elite, the “Relentless”: its hits grow with the health it has lost (up to 60% more), but it has 15% less health: finish it off fast!
  • During combat, the Relentless’s label shows the bonus of its hits (“+30%”… up to “+60%”): you can see the danger rise.
  • New pet, the “Weasel”: it brings dodge and ripostes, which no other pet gave.
  • New pet, the “Hummingbird”: your click strikes hit harder (up to 100% more at level 10).
  • New kind of quest: catch shooting stars, for those who have already caught one; the estimated time counts down to the next star.
  • Mini mode: hovering the monster, its tooltip shows its kind of elite and its effect, or the boss’s abilities.
  • Mini mode: during a rare event with a long name, the remaining seconds stay visible.
  • Bestiary: on a laptop screen, the page no longer scrolls when monster names wrap onto two lines (French, Spanish, Portuguese, Polish, Russian).
  • Strip on a laptop screen: an elite’s label during combat and the name of a spell cast at the start of a fight no longer cover the information at the top.
  • New achievement: “More Relentless Than Them (25 relentless)”, for 25 Relentless defeated.

Your save carries over: just install the new version over the old one.

Thanks for playing!
