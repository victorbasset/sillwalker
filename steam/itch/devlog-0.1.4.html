Sillwalker 0.1.4 is out! Here is what's new since 0.1.3:

  • Champions take center stage: a new quest asks you to defeat a champion (epic chest, sometimes an egg), and it keeps progressing while you're away, with an estimated time. A new achievement, Bane of Champions, rewards 25 of them; the hero cheers each one, with its own little fanfare.
  • Four new rare events: the Pale Moon (for 30 seconds every monster arrives with half its life, bosses included, under a washed-out sky and a white moon), Echoing Blades (every hit strikes twice), the Spring of Life (the hero regains 4% of max HP every second) and Buried Treasure (every victory may leave a treasure chest).
  • Three new elites: the Regenerating heals 3% of its life every second (a green +N shows it) unless it is burning or poisoned, the Venomous can poison the hero for a few seconds (never lethal), and the Explosive deals 6% of the hero's max HP when it dies (never lethal); a new achievement, Bomb Disposal, rewards 25 of them.
  • Daily blessings: six new ones (Day of Shadows, Day of Champions, Day of Giants: +50% damage against bosses, Day of Momentum: +100% on the first hit and click hits, Day of Blood: +5% life steal and +25% critical damage, Day of Arcana: spells recharge 50% faster), each blessing you live through gives +1% experience forever, and the Bestiary shows today's and tomorrow's.
  • Four new pets: the Froglet, whose croaking draws elites (+2% per level), the Stag Beetle, whose mandibles go for the bosses' weak spots (+3% damage against them per level), the Magpie, who spots treasure chests (+6% per level), and the Cricket, whose song draws rare events (+5% per level). And in the Shop, Hunting Incense: twice as many elites for 10 minutes, for those who want the risk (never rebought automatically).
  • Duo on the strip: a ranged hero now lets their melee companion walk in front.
  • Your companion's spells, in full: Hero > Spells & attack now shows them as large cards, to the right of your hero's, with their real (half) power; the hero card lists them too.
  • A real duo: a melee companion stands in front of a ranged hero and takes part of the blows (damage taken −15%); a ranged companion covers a melee hero with their shots (attack +15%).
  • Small comforts: an item bought at the shop that makes your hero stronger is equipped right away; after the flower, “Spend automatically” follows your most advanced line; the map shows your hero's head right on their zone.
  • Health at a glance: your health and the current monster's now sit at the top of the strip, next to the zone, with shields shown in blue (can be turned off in Settings > Window).
  • Mastery bonuses, one click away: every mastery with a two-bonus milestone has its own “Choose a bonus” / “Bonus” button.
  • Languages: Russian now uses the same pixel font as the other languages (66 letters drawn for the game), and Portuguese is consistently Brazilian.
  • At a glance: the strip shows your active elixirs, scrolls and incense as small icons in front of your gold.
  • Two new kinds of quest: gain levels, and catch golden goblins; both progress on their own, even while you're away.
  • Return after an absence: the summary now counts items left on the ground (full bag) and the champions that fell.
  • Fairer events: the Wind Gust now truly doubles your speed, even late in the game, and the Goblin Festival shows its +15% gold bonus.
  • Fairer absences: Scrolls of Fortune and Knowledge, the Potion of Haste, the Four-leaf Clover and Hunting Incense now count while you're away; spending all points in the great graph no longer freezes the game.
  • Many fixes in all 10 languages.

Your save carries over: just install the new version over the old one.

Thanks for playing!
