Sillwalker 0.1.10 is out! A new daily blessing that speeds up quests, a new rare event where elites become champions, a 24th pet that finishes off the wounded, a friendlier mini mode and a warmer welcome back. Here is what's new since 0.1.9:

  • New pet, the “Shrike”: your hits against enemies under 30% health hurt more (up to 30% more at level 10).
  • New daily blessing, “Zeal Day”: quests progress twice as fast, and their estimated times take it into account.
  • New rare event, the “Champions’ Challenge”: for 30 seconds, upcoming elites become champions, tougher but dropping an epic item.
  • Balance: the Mage, the hero who went the least far, has a bit more health (5% below normal instead of 15%).
  • Mini mode: the tooltips of Poupouce and of a champion also tell what they drop when they fall.
  • Returning: if your absence went past midnight, the summary announces the new day’s blessing.
  • Zeal Day: before quests open, its tooltip says they arrive after the first few minutes of play.
  • Translations: names that were getting mixed up are now distinct (a boss ability and a kind of elite in German and Polish, two spells, a power and a gear set in Chinese, Korean and Polish).

Your save carries over: just install the new version over the old one.

Thanks for playing!
