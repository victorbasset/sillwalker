Sillwalker 0.1.11 is out! Two new pets, the Wasp and the Woodpecker, a Pet Party rare event, a daily blessing for forging and a Bestiary that keeps count of every rare event and blessing you've lived. Here is what's new since 0.1.10:

  • New pet, the Wasp: your first hit in each fight strikes harder.
  • New pet, the Woodpecker: each hit in a row hurts a little more.
  • New rare event, “Pet Party”: for 30 seconds, your pet gains 10 times more experience.
  • New daily blessing, “Forge Day”: forging costs half as much that day.
  • Bestiary: each rare event you’ve lived shows how many times, and each daily blessing how many days you’ve had it.
  • Bestiary: the rare events title shows the total you’ve lived, to follow the 100-event achievement.
  • New achievement: “Witness of Wonders (100 rare events)”, a goal for long games.

Your save carries over: just install the new version over the old one.

Thanks for playing!
