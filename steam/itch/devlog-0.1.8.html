Sillwalker 0.1.8 is out! Champions you can actually beat, a richer return from absence, a full bag that never loses your best pieces, one-click purchases, readable huge numbers and a new rare event. Here is what's new since 0.1.7:

  • Champions: tougher than an elite, but weaker than their zone’s boss. Before, your hero lost almost every one of these fights.
  • Hunt quests: they only appear once you own the “Traveler's Map”, which takes you to the wanted monster in one click.
  • New rare event “Royal Favor”: for 30 seconds, every item found in combat is at least rare.
  • While you're away, defeated elites and champions now pay their gold and experience, just like in play.
  • Bag full: a legendary, set or forged piece is never sold or lost unless you ask (chests, Arms Valet).
  • An unlock announced outside the shop (Map, Pets, Skills…) can be bought in one click right there when you have the gold.
  • To-do: an affordable spell is learned in one click, without going to the heroes tab.
  • Very long games: very large numbers are written with letters (Ud, Dd… up to Vg) instead of scientific notation; on hover, the hero's tiles no longer show long rows of digits.
  • Returning: the absence summary now comes before other bubbles, and is no longer lost when the game starts in mini mode or minimized to its icon (launched with Windows): it waits until you open the strip.
  • On a laptop screen, spell icons, numbers and announcements no longer cover the information at the top of the strip.

Your save carries over: just install the new version over the old one.

Thanks for playing!
