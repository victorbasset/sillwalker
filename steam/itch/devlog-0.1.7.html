Sillwalker 0.1.7 is out! A lighter, freer Skills graph, a redone Talents tab, a theme for the final battle and a surprise for the ending. Here is what's new since 0.1.6:

  • Talents tab redone: the sheet follows the hover, nothing moves under the cursor anymore, each click shows its effect first, and Z undoes the last choice.
  • Lighter great Skills graph (222 nodes instead of 618): all three paths of each petal can be taken, and clicking a faraway node takes the shortest path.
  • Flower keystones no longer have a negative side; the game pace stays the same.
  • Zone 200: the final battle has its own music, “The Last Star”, and the final victory holds a surprise across your whole screen.
  • Actions with no way back (resetting, rising to the stars, erasing the save, selling a precious item) are confirmed in a window.
  • Keyboard: the arrows move through Talents, the bag and Skills; Z undoes; Escape and Tab behave as expected.
  • Clearer return from absence: “Things to do now” shows right away and can also suggest enlarging a full bag or visiting the Workshop.
  • Pets: “Take along” recalls a pet on an expedition and equips it in one click.
  • Achievements explained on hover, the three closest in gold; the Bestiary says where to find what is missing; the Logbook shows its next milestone.
  • Hit sounds follow the attack speed; softer volumes on first install; on Mac, the Dock menu shows in front of the strip.

Your save carries over: just install the new version over the old one.

Thanks for playing!
