An idle RPG that lives on the edge of your screen. Your hero walks a pixel-art strip above the taskbar, fights, loots and grows stronger while you work, play or watch videos.

Fact sheet

Developer
Victor Basset (solo developer, France)
Genre
Idle RPG, incremental, pixel art
Platforms
Windows, macOS (Apple silicon), Linux
Availability
itch.io (version 0.1.3) · Steam: coming soon
Languages
English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Polish, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean
  • 20 heroes to free, fighting in pairs, each with their own talents and unique spells
  • A 144-skill tree surrounded by a 618-node great graph of paths and synergies
  • Over 30 regions, 83 monsters, elites, golden goblins and bosses guarding relics
  • 15 rare events, 15 boss abilities, a different blessing every day
  • Progress continues while the game is closed (up to 8 hours)
  • Clicks pass through the strip; it hides in front of fullscreen games and videos
